Book One of the GOTNA Trilogy

The baby did not cry.

Dr. Evelyn Hart will do anything to save her daughter. Born blind, limbless, and silent, three-week-old Nunnally is failing — and the experimental gene therapy Evelyn injects in secret is her last act of desperate love.

But Evelyn's husband Marcus carries a different desperation home from his classified military post. Inside a concrete cell at Forge Laboratory, a teenage boy sits in permanent containment — not because he's dangerous, but because his body produces a compound that rewrites human biology through proximity. Through touch. Through love. Everyone who gets close becomes dependent. Everyone who leaves begins to die.

When sixteen-year-old Elara arrives carrying scars of her own, she discovers that the line between weapon and victim dissolved long ago. The boy isn't a monster. He's lonely. And the compound spreading through the Hart family doesn't cage them — it makes the cage feel like home.

A novel about the horrors we build from tenderness, the weapons we make of our children, and the bonds that kill us softly.

About the Author

Roguenealer Stevens

Roguenealer Stevens writes at the intersection of literary fiction and biological horror — stories where the science is real, the dread is quiet, and the monsters are made of love.

Womb of Shadows is the first novel in the GOTNA trilogy, a series that asks what happens when the military builds a weapon from a child's loneliness, and the child builds something back.

Based in Virginia. Working on Book Two.
