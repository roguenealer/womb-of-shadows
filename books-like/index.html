Recommendations

What to read after the books that broke you

Honest lists for readers of literary, biological, and cosmic horror — from Peter Neal, author of Womb of Shadows.

  1. Books like Annihilation

    Jeff VanderMeer

    If what stayed with you was the slow, clinical unraveling — a landscape that rewrites the people inside it, and prose that never raises its voice — start here.

  2. Books like Tender Is the Flesh

    Agustina Bazterrica

    Bazterrica's power is normalization: paperwork and procedure wrapped around atrocity. These books work the same nerve.

  3. Books like Bird Box

    Josh Malerman

    Malerman's trick was making the unseen lethal. These books all turn perception itself into the threat.

  4. Books like The Fisherman

    John Langan

    Langan proved cosmic horror hits hardest when it's about loss first and monsters second. Same rule here.
